## ABOUT KAMESKO SACCO SOCIETY

Kamesko Sacco Society is a member-owned financial cooperative located in Kansabet, Nandi County. We are committed to **catalyzing the industry, actualizing the dreams, and prospering together** with our members and the wider community.

We offer affordable funding through the mobilization of our resources. Our mission is simple: Together we grow, together we prosper. Your success is our success - together we build wealth that lasts.

## OUR RETURNS TO MEMBERS

- **Attractive Dividends** - We share our surplus with you
- **Interest on Savings** - Your savings grow with us
- **Linkages with Participating Partners** - Access more opportunities and value through our trusted partnerships
- **Member Empowerment** - We reinvest in you and our community
